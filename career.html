Analytics Practice Paid 6 Months
★ Permanent Posting Eligible

Analytics Consulting Intern

Turning complex operational and financial data into executive decisions.

Degree Required: B.Com / M.Com (Commerce / Finance)
Duration: 6 Months (Full-Time / Paid)
Target Sectors: Textile, Manufacturing & Hospitality
Key Stack: MS Excel, Python, SQL & AI Tools

Role Responsibilities:

  • Extract, clean, and validate transactional data from ERPs, production logs, and accounting books.
  • Design financial models, contribution margin analyses, and inventory turnover dashboards.
  • Write basic SQL queries and Python scripts to automate routine reporting and data aggregation.
  • Use generative AI prompts to synthesize market research, identify operational anomalies, and prepare summary briefs.
  • Collaborate with practice partners on executive presentations for C-level manufacturing clients.

Required Candidate Profile:

  • Graduating B.Com or M.Com student with a solid understanding of commerce, cost accounting, and financial statements.
  • Strong command of Microsoft Office, especially advanced Excel (VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables).
  • Familiarity with basic Python (pandas/dataframes) and SQL (SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY).
  • Curiosity to learn manufacturing cost drivers, textile supply dynamics, or hospitality P&L mechanics.
Strategy Practice Paid 6 Months
★ Permanent Posting Eligible

Strategy Consulting Intern

Architecting standard operating procedures and business growth roadmaps.

Degree Required: B.Com / M.Com (Commerce / Management)
Duration: 6 Months (Full-Time / Paid)
Target Sectors: Textile, Manufacturing & Hospitality
Key Stack: MS PowerPoint, Word, Excel, AI Prompts

Role Responsibilities:

  • Conduct workload assessments, process mapping, and bottleneck analysis on client shop floors.
  • Draft structured, easy-to-follow Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and RACI matrices for staff.
  • Assist in drafting Annual Business Plans, unit economics projections, and sales milestone trackers.
  • Leverage AI tools to accelerate documentation drafting, research industry benchmarks, and structure slide storylines.
  • Participate in client review workshops across textile processing units, manufacturing sites, and hospitality venues.

Required Candidate Profile:

  • B.Com or M.Com graduate with outstanding verbal clarity, structured reasoning, and documentation skills.
  • High proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint (executive slide structuring) and Microsoft Word.
  • Comfort reading business metrics, basic SQL/Python data tables, and interpreting operational dashboards.
  • Enthusiasm for fieldwork, understanding how factory floors produce goods and hotels deliver service quality.

Transparent Hiring Process

How We Select & Onboard You

Our hiring process moves quickly. Top candidates receive offers within 10 to 14 business days.

Stage 01

Application & Screening

Submit your details and resume below. Our consulting team reviews your academic track, degree (B.Com/M.Com), and analytical interests within 48 hours.

Stage 02

Practical Case Prompt

A short, real-world case prompt testing basic Excel logic, data interpretation, or an SOP scenario. You are encouraged to use modern AI tools to augment your answer.

Stage 03

Partner Interview

A 30-minute conversation with a practice director discussing your commerce interests, problem-solving mindset, and curiosity about manufacturing, textiles, or hospitality.

Stage 04

Offer & Paid 6M Start

Receive your 6-month paid internship offer letter. You'll be assigned a dedicated senior mentor with clear performance milestones for permanent full-time conversion (PPO).
